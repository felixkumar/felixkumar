Is this app compatible with Odoo Enterprise as well as Community?

Yes, the app works perfectly well with Odoo Enterprise (On-premise and Odoo.SH) as well as Community. Odoo Online (Cloud) does not allow installation of third-party apps and hence this app cannot be installed on Odoo Online.

Do I get free upgrades and support if I purchase the app?

If you purchase our app for Odoo Version 12 and prior, we will provide you access to all the versions up to 12 depending on their availability. Starting version 13, every version of the app will be sold separately. We offer free support for a duration of 90 days from the date of purchase. Kindly read our support policy to know the details.

I need an extra feature in this app. How can I request it?

You can contact us to request a customization in the app.

How many Walmart stores can I connect with a single Odoo Instance?

The Walmart Odoo Connector lets connect as many Walmart stores as you want with a single Odoo Instance.

Does the Walmart Odoo Connector allow two-way synchronization of Products?

Yes, you can not export Products from Odoo to Walmart as well as import Products from Walmart to Odoo. If the products are already present on Odoo as well as Walmart, you can establish a synchronization between them using the connector.

On what basis are the product synchronized in Walmart Odoo Connector?

The connector synchronizes products by matching the Internal Reference field in Odoo with the SKU field in Walmart. It is mandatory to have a unique SKU or Internal Reference for a product.

How do I manage product prices with the connector?

The connector allows you to update price from Odoo to Walmart via a pricelist. You can set up a pricelist per Walmart instance in Odoo and update the price to Walmart via the pricelist. You also import the prices from Walmart to Odoo, however, the prices will get imported to the pricelist and not directly to the product.

How do I manage stock updates with the connector?

The connector allows you to update stock from Odoo to Walmart either manually or automatically via a cron job. Stock levels get automatically adjusted in Odoo based on the imported orders. You can also import stock levels from Walmart to Odoo. The imported stock values will create an inventory adjustment entry in Odoo which upon validating will affect the actual stock levels. In the connector settings, you can choose one warehouse per Walmart Instance from which the stock is to be managed.

What kind of orders get imported to Odoo and how can I control it?

Any order on Walmart can be imported to Odoo if the connector has been configured correctly. Orders in Walmart have two order statuses such as ‘Shipped’, ‘Unshipped’. In the connector.

What if any of my product is not found in Odoo but it is there in Walmart. Can it create new product automatically in Odoo?

Yes. This connector can automatically create new product in Odoo if it is there in Walmart but not in your Odoo. Just check the box ‘Auto Create Offer Not found in Odoo’ while configuring marketplace.

I have submitted the feed to Walmart from Odoo. However, in some case informations are not shown in my Walmart account. Is there any error?

In this case, you can track submitted feed history by clicking on feed history box on Walmart Dashboard. This information is useful to technical persons rather than end users.

Does Walmart provides sandbox account?

Yes. Walmart provides the sandbox account to its seller. For that, first, seller needs to open the account with Walmart and then request its Sandbox account. More information can be available from Walmart support.

Do I have to pay recurring fees after I buy this app?

Yes, you will need to pay recurring fees in the form of IAP Packs for continuous usage of the app.

Does the app that I purchase from Odoo App Store include any features?

No, it is only a base app that enables you to avail our IAP service for the Walmart Odoo Connector. Buying and installing the base app of Odoo App Store will not let you use any of the features.
